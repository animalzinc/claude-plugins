# The usage tab is here

## Why we built it

Most dashboards die quietly—nobody decides to stop using them, they just stop. The usage tab shows when each dashboard was last opened, and by how many people.

In the beta, 30 percent of workspaces archived at least one dashboard in the first week. Another 12 percent assigned a new owner to one they kept.

## What it shows

Sort by last opened and the stale ones rise to the top—the same list every data lead has been meaning to make. The tab is free on the Team and Business plans and takes two minutes to read.
